Listening was impossible. These were chosen from source metadata, tags, stated durations and descriptions. BPM is given only where the source lists it. All seven files passed complete FFmpeg decoding; codec, sample peaks and checksums are in [audio-inspection.json](audio-inspection.json). Technical decoding does not establish how a track sounds. The owner must audition the files and the final mix.

The beds are the first 40 seconds of each source, encoded as 128 kbps stereo MP3 at 48 kHz. They have no baked fades or gain and are not guaranteed seamless loops. Effects are the unmodified, publicly linked HQ MP3 previews. No sign-in or login-gated download was used. Several assets peak near 0 dBFS, so use the gain/mix guidance in the video brief (content/video/video-brief.md in the production workspace).

C10 delivers a vertical SFX-only version and an identical-picture version with one auditioned bed. The site version starts muted in the player and carries an optional auditioned bed. Do not bundle an in-app trending track with these files. These files were copied unmodified into `video/assets/audio/` with this ledger (`rights.json`, whose `file` paths are relative to the original `content/video/` folder), `audio-inspection.json` and `proofs/`. Keep other capture, brand and font rights in their owning manifests.
